How RUBY Works

From diagnosis to disbursement, one trusted path

A fully auditable journey that protects patients and donors alike.

  1. 01 person

    Patient registers

    A patient creates their RUPID — the RUBY Universal Patient ID — with verified identity documents.

  2. 02 verified_user

    Verification

    AI-assisted checks flag inconsistencies. Documents are cross-checked by the attending hospital.

  3. 03 campaign

    Appeal is created

    A verified appeal with treatment plan, cost estimate and a clear funding target is published.

  4. 04 volunteer_activism

    Donors contribute

    Supporters fund cases directly in INR, with every transaction recorded on the prototype ledger.

  5. 05 local_hospital

    Hospital holds funds in escrow

    Funds stay in prototype escrow until a medical milestone is verified by the partner hospital.

  6. 06 account_balance_wallet

    Verified disbursement

    Approved milestones are disbursed to the hospital, and donors can see every rupee settled.
