How RUBY Works

A transparent path from diagnosis to disbursement

Every rupee is verified, escrowed, and publicly accounted for.

01

Patient gets a RUPID

Patients verify their identity through the multi-step flow (demographic, documents, OTP) and receive a unique RUBY Universal Patient ID with a verification score.

Get RUPID arrow_forward
02

Appeal is created

The patient builds a fund appeal: diagnosis, treatment plan, cost estimate, funding target, story and supporting documents.

Start an appeal arrow_forward
03

AI-assisted verification

An AI-assisted check flags inconsistencies in the medical documents. It scores the case as Low, Medium or High risk — the hospital decides, never the AI.

See the flow arrow_forward
04

Hospital verifies the case

The attending partner hospital confirms the diagnosis, treatment plan and cost estimate on the hospital dashboard before any money is asked for.

Hospital portal arrow_forward
05

Donors fund the case

Only verified, published cases appear publicly. Donors give in INR via simulated UPI / card / net banking, and each payment is recorded with a transaction ID.

Browse cases arrow_forward
06

Escrow → milestone → disbursement

Donations rest in prototype escrow. As treatment milestones are met, funds are released to the hospital — approved by the hospital and recorded publicly on the ledger.

View ledger arrow_forward

Built for trust, not for speed.
