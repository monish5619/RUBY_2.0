About RUBY

Medical crowdfunding, rebuilt around verification

RUBY is a prototype that demonstrates how AI-assisted verification, hospital partnerships, milestone escrow and a public ledger can restore trust in medical fundraising.

Why RUBY?

Traditional crowdfunding trusts the campaigner alone. RUBY puts three independent checks in the path: an AI-assisted document scan, an attending hospital's medical confirmation, and a human admin's final review — before a single rupee is solicited.

The escrow model

Donations do not go directly to the patient. They rest in prototype escrow and are released to the partner hospital against treatment milestones. This protects donors from misuse and patients from losing funds to non-medical expenses.

The transparency ledger

Every donation and disbursement carries a transaction ID and a mock block hash, visible to anyone. The demo ledger shows the design; a production deployment would anchor to a public chain.
